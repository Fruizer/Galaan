# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Friend groups (barkadas), cousins, and other social circles coordinating shared hangouts. Members use the app to settle plans that would otherwise remain tentative or disappear in group chats.

## Product Purpose

Galaan gives separate friend circles a shared calendar and event workspace for planning hangouts, confirming attendance, splitting shared expenses, and adding a playful signature to an RSVP. Success means members can see the plan, commitment level, venue, and cost without searching a group chat.

## Positioning

Galaan makes a plan visibly move from “Drawing” to “May Kulay Na” when enough members commit to its quorum, turning attendance into a shared, legible signal.

## Operating Context

Members open Galaan as needed while coordinating a social outing, on desktop or mobile web. A person may belong to multiple separate circles and switches between each circle’s calendar and events. Local MVP data represents three sample circles and is for interactive frontend evaluation only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Responsive browser app; no app-store install is required. Optional home-screen installation can come later.
- Circles have isolated calendars, members, and galas.
- The home view has an interactive monthly calendar and chronological lists for this month and later months.
- Gala status is derived from confirmed RSVP count and quorum.
- Members can RSVP with a pirma drawn on canvas, view venue and map links, inspect expense splits, and toggle paid status.
- Organizers can create a gala with a vibe, date, time, venue, quorum, and initial expenses.
- This requested MVP uses realistic local mock data and browser state, not a live backend or multi-user synchronization.
- Product-specific accessibility standard remains undecided; support keyboard use, visible focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion preferences in the MVP.

## Brand Commitments

The product is named Galaan. Its product language includes “barkada,” “gala,” “ambagan,” “pirma,” “Drawing,” and “May Kulay Na.” The user approved creating a fresh visual identity; no supplied logo, palette, or other brand assets exist in the starter project.

## Evidence on Hand

The product requirements and terminology are supplied in the project brief. No live customer data, testimonials, partner claims, or brand assets were supplied. Demo names, places, dates, headcounts, and expenses must read as illustrative mock data.

## Product Principles

- Make the current circle and its plans easy to scan.
- Show commitment transparently through headcount and quorum.
- Keep planning actions short and usable on a phone.
- Make shared costs and payment status understandable to every attendee.
- Treat each friend circle as separate product context.
